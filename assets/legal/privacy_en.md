# Privacy Policy — Crew Bites

**Last updated:** 2026-07-19  
**App:** Crew Bites (`com.i1988.crewbites`)  
**Contact:** i1988.support@gmail.com

## Summary

Crew Bites helps friends organize group food orders on a single phone.  
**Your data stays on your device.** We do not create accounts and we do not upload your names or food orders to our servers.

## What we store on your device

Using the app may save locally (via the device’s app storage / SharedPreferences):

- Friend names, emoji, and colors you add  
- Food items, quantities, optional prices, and notes  
- Bundle / menu names you create  
- Recent order history (last 3 orders)  
- App settings (theme, language, currency, prices on/off, tips seen)

You can clear custom names from **Settings**. Uninstalling the app removes local data (subject to your device’s backup rules).

## What we do **not** collect

- No user accounts or passwords  
- No advertising ID for ads (we show no ads)  
- No in-app purchases  
- No location tracking  
- No analytics SDKs shipping with this release  

## Sharing

If you tap **Share**, your device’s system share sheet may send an **order summary you chose to share** to another app (Messages, WhatsApp, email, etc.). That is under the privacy policy of the app you select—not ours.

## Network

Fonts and assets are bundled offline. Normal use does not require a network connection. Sharing uses the target app’s connection.

## Children

Crew Bites is a general utility. Do not enter sensitive personal data of children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

- Change language / theme / currency in Settings  
- Clear custom roster names  
- Stop using the app and uninstall to remove local data  

## Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. The “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s Privacy screen.
